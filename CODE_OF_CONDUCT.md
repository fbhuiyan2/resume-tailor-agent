# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be a person. Don't be a problem. Disagree on technical things, not on people.

## What's fine

- Disagreeing with a design choice, with reasoning.
- Saying "this PR is wrong because X".
- Closing your own PR after rethinking it.
- Pushing back on a maintainer's call (politely, once or twice).
- Not knowing something and asking.

## What's not

- Personal attacks, slurs, harassment, doxxing, sustained off-topic hostility.
- Sexual content in issues / PRs / discussions / commit messages.
- Spamming the repo (drive-by AI-generated PRs that don't compile or weren't tested count).
- Repeatedly arguing the same closed point.
- Demanding maintainer time. This is a side project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers can lock threads, hide comments, close PRs, and ban accounts. There's no committee — the call is the maintainer's. If you think a call was wrong, say so in the same thread; if it stays wrong, walk away and fork.

## Reporting

Email <fbhuiyan@anl.gov> for anything you don't want to post publicly. Don't @ people in unrelated issues.

## Scope

This applies to the repo: issues, PRs, discussions, commit messages, and any project-controlled space. Outside that, you're on your own.
