# Verb bank — strong, ATS-friendly action verbs

Lead every bullet with one of these. Past tense for completed work, present tense only for current ongoing roles. No "Responsible for", no "Worked on", no "Helped with" — those are weak.

## Built / shipped
Built, Shipped, Designed, Developed, Engineered, Implemented, Launched, Released, Delivered, Created, Constructed, Authored, Wrote, Coded, Programmed, Prototyped.

## Improved / optimized
Optimized, Reduced, Cut, Accelerated, Improved, Increased, Boosted, Doubled, Tripled, Halved, Streamlined, Refactored, Simplified, Consolidated, Tuned, Hardened.

## Automated / scaled
Automated, Scaled, Parallelized, Distributed, Migrated, Replatformed, Containerized, Orchestrated, Scheduled, Productionized, Operationalized.

## Data / analytics
Modeled, Ingested, Extracted, Transformed, Loaded, Cleaned, Normalized, Aggregated, Queried, Indexed, Visualized, Dashboarded, Reported, Forecasted, Analyzed, Mined, Profiled.

## Investigated / fixed
Debugged, Diagnosed, Resolved, Patched, Fixed, Investigated, Audited, Reviewed, Hardened, Secured, Validated.

## Led / collaborated (use sparingly without team context)
Led, Owned, Drove, Coordinated, Mentored, Onboarded, Reviewed, Partnered.

## Researched / decided
Researched, Evaluated, Benchmarked, Compared, Selected, Recommended, Specified, Documented.

## Migrated / integrated
Migrated, Integrated, Connected, Synced, Replicated, Bridged, Adapted.

---

## Banned weak openers
- Responsible for / Tasked with / In charge of
- Worked on / Helped with / Assisted with / Participated in
- Was involved in / Played a role in
- Utilized (use "Used") / Leveraged (use "Used")

If a bullet starts with one of those, rewrite from the outcome backward.
